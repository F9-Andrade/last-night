# LAST NIGHT — field provisions

The eight `food-*.svg` icons are original vector artwork authored for LAST NIGHT in this update. They contain hand-composed stepped shapes, faceted metal, intact seals, faded fictional labels, dents and wear. No external image, trademark, icon pack, font file or licensed asset was downloaded or embedded.

Items: Santa Luz canned beans, Reserva 04 preserved meat, Costa sardines, Pomar peaches, Reserva Seca crackers, Lote 07 field ration, Fonte sealed drinking water and Faísca soda. These are fictional packaging names for this game.
